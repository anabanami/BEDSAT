\chapter{Aims}\label{aims}
\textbf{BedSAT: Antarctica} is a modelling approach that combines satellite observations of the ice surface, airborne radar surveys of the bed, and numerical ice flow modelling. It is physically informed by observed bed morphometry statistics that feed into synthetic bedrock generation for Ice-sheet and Sea-level System Model (ISSM)~\cite{Larour_2012} ice-sheet models. With BedSAT we aim to use machine learning to model bed-to-surface transfer from physics-based simulations over these synthetic beds. The goal is to invert the bed-to-surface transfer, so that observed surface expression from real satellite data can be mapped back to bed topography and generate a new Antarctic bed topography product with higher accuracy and resolution.

\section{Project Deliverables}
\begin{itemize}
    \item\textbf{Paper 1 — ODSA}: develop Observational Data Spectral Analysis, a physically informed spectral framework for quantifying Antarctic subglacial landscape characteristics and classifying bed morphology into archetypes for ice sheet modelling.
    \item\textbf{Paper 2 — Bedrock factory}: design a synthetic bed topography generator for Antarctic landscapes that takes morphometric statistics from ODSA as inputs and produces bed realisations at ice sheet model resolution.
    \item\textbf{Paper 3 — BedSAT: Antarctica}: as described above.
\end{itemize}
