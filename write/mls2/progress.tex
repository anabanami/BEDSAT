\section{Key research achieved (ODSA)}

ODSA operates on the along-track picks published with Bedmap3 instead of a gridded product, which allows us to measure the survey data directly and avoid the interpolation issues described in Ch.~\ref{review}. The tracks used are visible in Fig.~\ref{fig:Bedmap+Ockenden_map}. 
The ODSA pipeline splits tracks into segments, cuts them into $50~\mathrm{km}$ windows (when possible) with a $25\mathrm{km}$ step, applies a Hann taper, and fits the spectrum over $250~\mathrm{m}$ to $50~\mathrm{km}$ using a Lomb--Scargle periodogram. The morphometric statistics obtained from the RES data is checked against an 11-element landscape vector~\ref{tab:axes, tab:descriptors}. Every vector classifier has a $\pm 2\sigma$ uncertainty envelope, so no axis is assumed to be exact. The resultant vector is compared to a literature-based catalogue of 11 landscape archetypes. ODSA reports when a window matches more than one archetype, the pipeline names the axis responsible and the observable needed to break the tie.

For the seven regions analysed~\ref{fig:7regions} we found a total of 651 windows. Our main results include: landscape class is bounded by a transect but a single landscape class is not always identified with only RES data. Of the 11 catalogue entries, the median number still admissible per window is 2. The windows split is 402 degenerate, 135 resolved, 30 resolved-with-external, 84 out-of-catalogue.
We validated that our hypothesis that No single statistic identifies class~\ref{fig:hypothesis_test}.

\section*{Data Management}

Data will be published adhering to FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), ensuring transparency and accessibility. The final bed topography datasets will be published at the Australian Antarctic Data Centre (AADC) under an open source licence. All production model outputs will be published with unique DOIs at repositories aligned with the corresponding papers. Model outputs will be archived to tape at NCI using existing SAEF resources, as well as backed up to storage available through Monash MASSIVE M3 account aligned with project supervisor Dr McCormack. All papers published through this project will be open source, and tier 1 journals will be targeted.
